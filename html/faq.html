Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about StudentHub and its features.

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a centralized student portal that provides students with access to important academic and campus-related information.

How do I register?

Click the Register button and complete the registration form with your required student information.

How can I join an event?

Open the Events page, select an event, and follow the registration instructions.

How do I update my profile?

Open your Profile page and select Edit Profile to update your information.
